SLEEP · REPAIR RATIONALE
🛌 Why the Nap Is Capped
[+] Expand

Right now: wake 5:30–6:00 AM, sleep 12:30–1:30 AM → 4–5 hrs. Goal: 7–8 hrs. This gap is the #1 threat to everything else in this system — sleep debt quietly wrecks retention, focus, and mood before you even notice it happening.

The "sleep after school" instinct is partly right — but capped. A long, unstructured post-school sleep (1–2+ hrs) is likely why bedtime drifts to 12:30–1:30 AM — you're not tired enough at 10:30 PM because you already slept once. The fix isn't removing the nap, it's shrinking and fixing it, then rebuilding night sleep gradually.

Why this works: the nap is capped short enough to stay a recharge, not a second sleep cycle — so your body is still ready for sleep pressure to build again by night. The real lever is moving your hardest deep-work into early-to-mid evening instead of post-11 PM, so late-night study stops being the reason bedtime slips.
