## 🛌 First: Fixing the Sleep Deficit (Read This Before Anything Else)

Right now: wake 5:30–6:00 AM, sleep 12:30–1:30 AM → **4–5 hrs**. Goal: 7–8 hrs. This gap is the #1 threat to everything else in this system — sleep debt quietly wrecks retention, focus, and mood before you even notice it happening.

**The "sleep after school" instinct is partly right — but capped.** A long, unstructured post-school sleep (1–2+ hrs) is likely *why* bedtime drifts to 12:30–1:30 AM — you're not tired enough at 10:30 PM because you already slept once. The fix isn't removing the nap, it's **shrinking and fixing it**, then rebuilding night sleep gradually.

**📅 3-Phase Bedtime Repair**

| Phase | Weeks | Post-School Nap | Target Bedtime | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1–2 | 25–30 min (alarm-locked, no exceptions) | 12:00–12:15 AM | ~5.5–6 hrs |
| 2 | 3–4 | 20 min | 11:30 PM | ~6.5–7 hrs |
| 3 | 5+ | 15–20 min (optional, only if genuinely wiped) | 10:30–11:00 PM | ~7–7.5 hrs |

**Why this works:** the nap is capped short enough to stay a recharge, not a second sleep cycle — so your body is still ready for sleep pressure to build again by night. The real lever is moving your *hardest deep-work* into early-to-mid evening (your stated peak) instead of post-11 PM, so late-night study stops being the reason bedtime slips.

## 📘 SCHOOL DAY

```
🌅 Wake (5:30–6:00 AM)
   ↓
💧 Hydrate + Freshen Up + Quick Stretch (5 min)
   ↓
🧠 2-Min Recall — glance at yesterday's error log / key formula sheet (no new learning, just priming)
   ↓
🎒 Get Ready + Commute to School
   🎧 Use this window for chapter audiobooks (NotebookLM etc.) — passive revision, zero extra time cost
   ↓
🏫 SCHOOL (8:00 AM – 4:00/5:00 PM)
   ↓
🚌 Commute Home
   🎧 Same audiobook slot, or just decompress — don't force focus here
   ↓
😴 RECOVERY BLOCK (mandatory, not optional)
   • 25–30 min power nap (alarm set, no snoozing) OR quiet decompression if not tired
   • Light snack + water
   ↓
📝 Homework / School Assignments (30–60 min)
   ↓
🏋️ Fitness Block (30–35 min)
   📘 Running/endurance
   • Push-ups + Pull-up progression
   • Core work
   • Mobility/stretch (2x/week swap to pure mobility+recovery day)
   ↓
🍽️ Dinner + Family Time (30 min, phone away)
   ↓
📘 DEEP WORK BLOCK 1 — Hardest Subject (60–90 min)
   Rotates per Weekly Subject Wheel below
   • Concept learning from lecture/notes
   • NCERT problems
   • PYQs where applicable
   • Error log entry
   ↓
☕ Break (10–15 min) — stretch, hydrate, NO phone
   ↓
📗 DEEP WORK BLOCK 2 — Moderate Subject (45–75 min)
   Same structure: concept → practice → error log
   ↓
☕ Break (10–15 min)
   ↓
📙 LIGHT BLOCK — Revision / CS / English / NDA GAT (30–45 min)
   Rotates per Weekly Subject Wheel
   ↓
🛠️ Skill / Project Block (0–30 min, flexible — first to shrink if day runs long)
   Coding · DSA · Robotics · Reading · GeetaDiva/ExamForge
   ↓
🎮 Leisure — guilt-free (30–45 min)
   Music / anime / YouTube / quiet time
   ↓
🗒️ Planning + Reflection (10 min)
   • Tomorrow's priority subject
   • One-line: what worked / what didn't today
   ↓
🌙 Night Routine + Digital Detox (15 min)
   Phone away, lights low, wind down
   ↓
😴 SLEEP (target per current phase — see repair table above)
```

## ⚡ BUSY / SPECIAL DAY *(Competitions, Travel, Events, Sick Days, Low-Energy)*

This is a **triage template** — the goal is protecting the floor, not hitting the ceiling.

```
🌅 Wake (whatever time the day demands)
   ↓
💧 Hydrate + 2-Min Recall (non-negotiable — keeps the thread alive)
   ↓
🏃 Micro-Movement (10 min) — mobility/stretch only, skip full fitness session
   ↓
[ THE DAY'S ACTUAL DEMAND — competition / travel / event / rest ]
   ↓
📘 ONE Priority-Subject Touch (30–45 min minimum, wherever it fits)
   Even just error-log review or 10 problems — the point is continuity, not volume
   ↓
📝 Homework only if truly unavoidable
   ↓
🌙 Digital Detox Before Sleep (still enforced — this is cheap and high-value even on chaotic days)
   ↓
😴 SLEEP — protect this above all else today
```

**Rule for busy days: sleep and the one priority-subject touch are the only two non-negotiables. Everything else is optional.**

## 🚑 Fallback Protocols (Recovery Without Guilt)

| Situation | What To Do |
|---|---|
| **Low motivation** | Do 10 min of the easiest task on the list — momentum beats willpower. Don't skip the whole day. |
| **Unexpected guests/family event** | Protect only: 2-min recall + one priority-subject touch + sleep. Everything else is skippable. |
| **School runs overtime** | Cut skill block first, then Deep Block 2 duration — never cut sleep or the recall block. |
| **Travel day** | Use commute-style audiobook listening as your only "study." That's enough for one day. |
| **Feeling sick** | Rest fully. One glance at notes if genuinely up for it — otherwise zero guilt, full stop. |
| **Missed a session entirely** | Do NOT try to "make it up" by stacking tomorrow. Just resume the normal wheel — missed rotations repeat next week anyway. |

**The system is designed to survive being broken. A missed day is not a failed system — only giving up on resuming is.**

## 🗂️ Weekly System

**✅ Weekly Reset Checklist (Sunday evening)**
- [ ] Error logs reviewed across all subjects touched this week
- [ ] Next week's Subject Wheel confirmed (no changes unless exam approaching)
- [ ] Fitness progression checked (add 1 rep/round if last week felt manageable)
- [ ] Sleep phase checked — ready to tighten bedtime by 30 min?

**📊 Weekly Review — 3 Questions**
1. Which subject got the least real attention this week?
2. What's one thing that broke the schedule, and what's the fix?
3. Energy levels this week: consistently low / mixed / good?

**🎯 Priority Matrix (use when a day gets overloaded)**

| | Urgent | Not Urgent |
|---|---|---|
| **Important** | Do first (exam-linked deadlines) | Schedule normally (Wheel subjects) |
| **Not Important** | Delegate/skip if possible | Cut without guilt (this is the skill block, leisure overflow) |

**📈 Habit Tracker (simple daily tick)**

`Wake time on target · Fitness done · Deep Block 1 completed · Deep Block 2 completed · Digital detox before sleep · Sleep 7hrs+`

**🗓️ Monthly Reflection**
- Syllabus completion % per subject vs. plan
- Sleep phase progress (which phase are you actually in now?)
- One habit to double down on, one to drop

## 📌 Notes on Design

- **No fixed clock times on purpose** — if your day shifts 30–60 min, the block order still holds.
- **NDA GAT builds gradually** — it's in the Light Block rotation now, and will need a dedicated Deep Block once Boards syllabus crosses ~70%.
- **Mock tests activate automatically** once any subject crosses 70–80% syllabus completion — don't force them before that.
- **This is standalone** — separate from your Operating Manual and Mission Control, but nothing here conflicts with either if you want to merge later.
